🚫 Forbidden / Prohibited Dates in Islam (Ayyam e Mamnu)

Fasting is prohibited / disliked on these days according to Sunnah

  • 1st Shawwal - Eid ul Fitr: Fasting is Haram. Day of celebration after Ramadan.
  • 10th Dhul Hijjah - Eid ul Adha: Fasting is Haram. Day of Sacrifice.
  • 11th, 12th, 13th Dhul Hijjah - Ayyam at-Tashreeq: Fasting is Haram (except for Hajji who cannot sacrifice).
  • Friday only as single fast: Makrooh to fast only Friday alone without fasting a day before or after.
  • Day of Doubt - 30th Shaban: Disliked to fast if intention is to be cautious for Ramadan.
  • Continuous fasting without break (Sawm al-Wisal): Forbidden.
  • Wife fasting voluntary without husband's permission: Not allowed when husband is present.
Note: This information is for general awareness. Always confirm with authentic scholar. Purpose is to avoid mistakes in Ibadah.

📅 Important Islamic Dates 2025-26

  • Ramadan - Fasting Month
  • Laylatul Qadr - Last 10 nights of Ramadan
  • Eid ul Fitr - 1 Shawwal
  • Day of Arafah - 9 Dhul Hijjah (Recommended fast for non-Hajis)
  • Eid ul Adha - 10 Dhul Hijjah
  • Ashura - 10 Muharram
