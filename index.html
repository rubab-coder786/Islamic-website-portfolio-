🌸 HAYA Kya Hai?

HAYA (حیاء) Arabic ka lafz hai jiska matlab hai Sharam, Paakizgi aur Iman ki khoobsurti. Hadees me hai: "Haya Iman ka hissa hai."

Jab dil me Haya aati hai to zindagi me sukoon aur khushi khud ba khud aa jati hai. Isi liye hum ne naam rakha HAYA BRINGS HAPPINESS - Kyunki Haya dil ko khush rakhti hai.
